Privacy Policy — ThreadsResearch

ThreadsResearch is a personal research application used to analyze publicly available Threads posts for financial-market research.

The application may access publicly available Threads profile and post information through the official Threads API, including:

  • public post text
  • publication timestamps
  • post identifiers
  • usernames
  • permalinks

The application does not sell personal information.

The application does not use collected information for advertising.

The application does not share collected information with third parties.

API access tokens and authentication credentials are stored locally and are not published or shared.

Data obtained through the Threads API is used solely for research and analysis.

Data Deletion

Requests concerning data stored by ThreadsResearch can be sent to the contact email above. Relevant stored research data will be reviewed and deleted where applicable.
